# Lean Startup

## What It Is
A methodology for developing businesses and products under conditions of extreme uncertainty, developed by Eric Ries and rooted in lean manufacturing and agile development principles. Lean Startup replaces the traditional "build it and they will come" approach with a validated learning loop: Build → Measure → Learn (BML). Instead of building a complete product based on assumptions, teams create a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) to test the riskiest assumptions as quickly and cheaply as possible, measure the results, and use data to decide whether to Pivot (change direction) or Persevere (continue the current approach). The methodology applies to both new ventures and innovation programs within established organizations.

## When to Use
- New product development under significant market or technical uncertainty
- Innovation programs within enterprise clients where assumptions about user demand are unvalidated
- When a client is about to invest heavily in a product concept without evidence it will be adopted
- Structuring a discovery phase: what hypotheses need to be tested before committing to full delivery?
- When scope decisions are driven by opinions rather than evidence

## Key Concepts
- **Build-Measure-Learn (BML) Loop**: The core cycle — build the smallest thing that tests the hypothesis, measure the outcome, learn whether the hypothesis was validated or invalidated. The goal is to complete the loop as quickly as possible
- **Minimum Viable Product (MVP)**: The minimum experiment needed to test the most critical assumption. Not the minimum shippable product — the minimum learning vehicle. MVPs range from a landing page to a concierge service to a functional prototype. Scope is defined by the hypothesis, not by feature completeness
- **Hypothesis-Driven Development**: Every feature investment starts with a hypothesis — "We believe that [customer segment] will [do X] because [motivation]. We'll know we're right when [measurable outcome]." This replaces assumptions with testable claims
- **Validated Learning**: The only meaningful output of early-stage product development is learning that validates or invalidates assumptions. Features are outputs; learning is the outcome
- **Pivot vs. Persevere**: At each BML cycle review, teams decide whether evidence supports continuing (persevere) or changing a core assumption (pivot). Pivot types include: customer segment pivot, problem pivot, solution pivot, technology pivot, channel pivot
- **Innovation Accounting**: Traditional financial metrics (revenue, profit) don't apply to early-stage products. Innovation accounting tracks leading indicators of product-market fit: activation rates, retention cohorts, engagement depth
- **Batch Size**: Lean manufacturing insight applied to product: smaller batches of work cycle through the BML loop faster, reducing wasted investment in unvalidated features
- **Lean Startup in Enterprise**: The methodology adapts to enterprise contexts where "launch and learn" conflicts with compliance, procurement, or brand risk. Enterprise MVP = limited rollout to a pilot group, controlled experiment, or simulation rather than public launch

## Method Application
Method applies Lean Startup principles in discovery phases and innovation programs. When a client has a technology investment that rests on unvalidated assumptions (customers will use this, this workflow will be adopted), Method structures a hypothesis-driven discovery phase before full delivery commitment. The deliverable is not a prototype — it's a validated learning report that informs the delivery decision.

## Consulting Insight
🎯 **Consulting Tool — Lean Startup**: The most valuable intervention from Lean Startup is introducing the word "hypothesis" into the client's vocabulary. When a product decision is framed as a belief ("we believe users want X") rather than a fact ("users want X"), it becomes testable rather than defensible. This shifts the conversation from opinions to evidence — and from "who has seniority" to "what does the data say." → `consulting-tools-repository/lean-startup.md`
