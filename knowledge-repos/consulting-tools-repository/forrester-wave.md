# Forrester Wave

## What It Is
Forrester Research's vendor evaluation methodology, parallel to Gartner's Magic Quadrant. The Forrester Wave evaluates vendors across three dimensions: Current Offering (product strength today), Strategy (roadmap and vision), and Market Presence (revenue, customer base, growth). Vendors are plotted on a chart with Current Offering on the x-axis and Strategy on the y-axis, in four categories: Leaders, Strong Performers, Contenders, and Challengers. The Wave includes detailed scoring rubrics across 20-30 evaluation criteria, making it more transparent than the Magic Quadrant in methodology.

## When to Use
- Vendor evaluation for technology categories where Forrester has stronger coverage than Gartner (B2B tech, customer experience, security)
- When a client wants a second analyst perspective alongside a Magic Quadrant
- When the Magic Quadrant doesn't cover the specific technology category
- Evaluating vendors in customer experience, marketing technology, and digital commerce categories (Forrester's historical strengths)

## Key Concepts
- **Current Offering**: Evaluated on product capabilities today — feature completeness, quality, integration depth. The horizontal axis
- **Strategy**: Evaluated on roadmap quality, market approach, partner ecosystem, pricing model. The vertical axis
- **Market Presence**: Size of the company, customer base, revenue, growth rate. Shown as bubble size — context for vendor viability, not a ranking dimension
- **Wave Methodology Transparency**: Forrester publishes the evaluation criteria and scoring methodology upfront. Vendors can see exactly what they're being scored on. This makes the Wave more transparent than the Magic Quadrant
- **Forrester vs. Gartner Coverage**:
  - Forrester historically stronger: customer experience technology, B2B/B2C marketing tech, identity management, cloud security
  - Gartner historically stronger: enterprise infrastructure, ERP/CRM platforms, data management
  - For modern digital commerce, CDPs, and customer data platforms: check both
- **Forrester Now Tech**: For emerging categories not yet mature enough for a full Wave, Forrester publishes "Forrester Now Tech" reports that inventory vendors without full scoring — useful for understanding a nascent market

## Method Application
Used alongside Magic Quadrant in vendor selection programs. For categories where both exist, comparing the two rankings often reveals interesting divergences — a vendor that is a Leader in Gartner and a Contender in Forrester signals a gap between the analyst communities worth investigating. Used as research input in pre-engagement and solutions phases.

## Consulting Insight
🎯 **Consulting Tool — Forrester Wave**: Use the Forrester Wave when you need a second opinion on the vendor landscape or when Gartner's coverage of your category is thin or dated. The Wave's published scoring criteria are a useful starting point for building your own vendor evaluation scorecard — even if you don't use Forrester's weighting, their criteria dimensions are well-researched.
